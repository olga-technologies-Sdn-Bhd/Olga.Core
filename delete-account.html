Delete your Ol-ga account

Ol-ga app by Olga Technologies Sdn Bhd · Last updated: 29 October 2026

In the app

  1. Open the Ol-ga app and sign in.
  2. Tap Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

Email privacy@ol-ga.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Ol-ga account". We may ask you to confirm it is you before we delete anything.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile: name, email, phone number, headline, role, company, interests and profile picture.
  • Your goals and matching data, matches, connection requests and commits.
  • Your chats and the photos, files and voice notes you sent.

What is kept, and for how long

We delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days of your request. We keep only records we must keep longer by law, or to resolve disputes and safety reports (for example a report you made or received), and only for as long as that needs.

See our Privacy Policy for more.

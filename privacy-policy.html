Olga Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains how Olga Technologies Sdn Bhd ("Olga", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects your personal data when you use the Olga mobile app and related services (the "Service"). We process personal data in line with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) and other laws that apply.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: your name, email address and/or phone number, and the one-time codes used to sign in.
  • Profile data: headline, role, company, industry, interests, profile picture and any other details you add to your profile.
  • Matching data: what you are looking for and what you offer (your "intents"), and the matches, connection requests and commits you make.
  • Event data: events you register for or check in to, and when you use Live Mode at an event.
  • Messages and files: chat messages, photos, documents and voice notes you send to other members.
  • Consent records: which policies you accepted or declined, and when.
  • Device data: device identifiers, app version, operating system and a push notification token, used to deliver the Service and notifications.
  • Location and nearby devices: only when you use features that need them (for example, finding people near you at an event), and only with your permission.

2. App permissions

The app asks for permission before using:

  • Camera and photos: to take or pick a profile picture or a photo to send in chat.
  • Microphone: to record voice notes.
  • Files and storage: to attach documents and save files you download.
  • Location and Bluetooth: for event and nearby features.
  • Notifications: to tell you about new matches, requests and messages.

You can turn these off at any time in your device settings. Some features will not work without them.

3. How we use your data

  • To create and run your account and sign you in securely.
  • To suggest relevant people to connect with. We use automated processing, including AI language models, to compare your intents and profile with other members' and to explain why you matched.
  • To deliver messages, files and notifications.
  • To keep the Service safe: files you send are automatically scanned for malware and harmful content, and we act on reports and blocks.
  • To run events, measure how the Service is used and improve it.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Who can see your data

  • Other members: your profile is visible to other members according to your visibility setting. Messages and files are visible only to the people in the conversation.
  • Event organisers: may see that you registered for or attended their event.
  • Service providers: we use trusted providers to host and run the Service (for example, Microsoft Azure for cloud hosting, storage and AI services, and email or SMS providers for sign-in codes). They process data only on our instructions.
  • Authorities: when required by law or to protect the safety of our members.

Our main servers are in Malaysia. Some providers may process data in other countries, with appropriate safeguards.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Access, correct or update your profile in the app.
  • Hide your profile, block members, and withdraw consent for matching or Live Mode at any time in the app.
  • Delete messages you sent.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to delete your account and data, by contacting us (see section 9).

6. Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where we must keep some records longer by law or to resolve disputes. Uploaded files that are never sent are deleted automatically.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is restricted to authorised staff and systems. No service is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the authorities of a breach where the law requires.

8. Children

The Service is for professionals aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy
